REBOOT RUSH

The internet is DOWN! Fix all 8 servers before the timer hits 0:00.

  1. 1
    MOVE around the facility.
    Keyboard: W A S D or arrow keys. Phone: the on-screen arrows.
  2. 2
    COLLECT chips and cables by walking over them.
    Each repair uses 1 chip + 2 cables. Check your PACK at the top.
  3. 3
    FIND A BROKEN SERVER (blinking red light) and stand next to it. Press E or Space (phone: the A button).
  4. 4
    SOLVE THE PUZZLE: tap a cable, then the port with the same color and shape. Match them all!
    Done = the light turns green and a service comes back online.
  5. !
    AVOID the flashing zap plates and roaming spark bots. Each zap costs you 5 seconds. A wrong port costs 2 seconds.

TIP: Grab a few chips and cables first, then repair servers one room at a time. It gets harder after each repair!
