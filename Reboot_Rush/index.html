REBOOT RUSH

Recent technology outages have disrupted banking services, Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) systems and Microsoft Outlook, highlighting just how dependent our world is on technology. In Reboot Rush, you take on the role of an IT technician racing against the clock to restore these essential services. Can you get the world back online before time runs out?

The internet is DOWN! Beat 3 levels, one outage at a time: Outlook Mail, then Banking, then the FAA Network. You have 3:00 in total.

  1. 1
    MOVE around the server room.
    Keyboard: W A S D or arrow keys. Phone: the on-screen arrows.
  2. 2
    COLLECT memory chips and network cables by walking over them.
    Each repair uses 1 chip + 2 cables. Check your PACK at the top.
  3. 3
    FIND A BROKEN SERVER (blinking red light) and stand next to it. Press E or Space (phone: the A button).
  4. 4
    SOLVE THE PUZZLE: tap a cable, then the port with the same color and shape.
    Fix every server in the room to clear the level. Clear a level to earn +25 seconds!
  5. !
    DODGE the ANGRY PEOPLE who are mad about the outage, and the flashing zap plates. Each hit costs 5 seconds. A wrong port costs 2 seconds.

TIP: Each level has more servers, more angry people and bigger puzzles. Grab parts first, then repair!

SERVICE BOARD

    MOVE: WASD / ARROWS   FIX: E / SPACE
