Enrollment Trends

How many students are in your major?

Search for a major to see how its enrollment has changed from Fall 2016 to Fall 2025. Add up to four majors to compare them.

      About the data: Fall headcount by major, from university data request DR12573 (Fall 2016–Fall 2025). A major's total adds up every college and degree program that lists it, graduate programs included.

      Programs get renamed, split and retired over time, so a line that starts or stops partway may reflect a name change rather than a real change in students. Names that differed only by “Raikes program only” wording were combined.
