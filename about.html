About

My story

Ever since I can remember, I’ve loved telling stories. At my childhood home, you can still find stacks of journals filled with half-written fiction I wrote growing up. I was captivated by how simple words can come together to create something much bigger than what appears on the page.

Storytelling doesn’t just captivate. It can create real change in a community.

That realization led me to journalism, where I get to combine creativity with purpose. At the University of Nebraska–Lincoln I major in journalism and broadcasting, with a minor in theatre. I’ve learned to tell stories that are accurate and deeply human, whether I’m covering campus events, national politics, local businesses or a community recovering from disaster.
